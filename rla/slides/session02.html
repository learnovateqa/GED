Summarize in one sentence

A good summary gives the main idea and the most important details. It is short, and it does not add your opinion.

  1. Who or what is the text about?
  2. What is the main point?
  3. What are one or two key details?

Inference: read the clues

An inference is an idea the text does not say directly. You put the clues together.

Clue→+ Clue→= Inference
Remember thisAn inference must be supported by the text. It is not a guess.

Point of view and tone

  • Point of view: what the author thinks about the topic.
  • Tone: the feeling in the words: hopeful, angry, worried, serious.
X Weak word choiceThe new park is fine.Neutral tone.
✓ Strong word choiceThe new park is a gift to every family in our town.Warm, positive tone. The author strongly approves.

Words in context

You meet a new word. Do not stop. Look around it.

  • Definition: the sentence explains it.
  • Example: the sentence gives examples.
  • Opposite: a word like *but* shows the opposite.
  • Test it: put your meaning in the sentence. Does it make sense?

Reading a story

  • Character: Who is it? What do they want? How do they change?
  • Setting: Where and when?
  • Plot: What happens, and in what order?
  • Theme: What lesson does the story teach?
Common mistakeGiving the theme as one word, like "family". A theme is a sentence: "Family can help us through hard times."
