What is RLA?

Reasoning Through Language Arts is one of the four GED® subjects.

It tests three things:

  • Reading: do you understand the text?
  • Arguments: can you judge a claim and its evidence?
  • Language: can you use correct written English?

Never leave a blank

Common mistakeLeaving a question with no answer. A blank answer is always wrong.
  1. Remove the answers that cannot be right.
  2. Choose the best answer that is left.
  3. Go on to the next question.

What you read

  • Informational texts: articles, letters, workplace notices, speeches.
  • Literary texts: stories with characters, feelings and choices.
Remember thisYou do not need to know the topic. Every answer comes from the passage.

What you write

One essay. 45 minutes.

  1. Read two passages with two different arguments.
  2. Decide which argument has more convincing evidence.
  3. Explain why. Use evidence from both passages.

The essay is not about your opinion

✗ Weak responseI think bicycle lanes are a good idea because I like cycling.This is the writer's own opinion.
✓ Strong responsePassage 1 gives the stronger argument. It uses accident numbers from three cities.This judges the evidence in the passages.

The four-step method

Read the question→Read the passage→Find the evidence→Answer

Use these four steps for every reading question.

Use your time well

✗ Weak planI will stay on a hard question until I get it.
✓ Strong planI choose my best answer, mark the question, and come back if I have time.
  • Check the clock after each passage.
  • Keep a few minutes at the end of the essay to check it.
